About

behind it.

I'm Suzanne “Aden” Kemp — a professional bagpiper with nearly three decades of experience performing the Great Highland Bagpipe throughout New Mexico, across the United States, and internationally. Playing since 1999, I have served as a competitive pipe band leader directing performances, masterclass instruction, and musical development. From solemn remembrance services and military honors to joyous wedding celebrations, live bagpipe music carries a unique resonance that echoes through history. Every performance is approached with absolute musical excellence, authenticity, and profound respect for your occasion.
