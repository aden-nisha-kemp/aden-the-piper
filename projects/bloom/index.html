overview

Commencement ceremonies celebrate years of hard work, intellect, and ambition. The bold, resonant timbre of the Great Highland Bagpipe leads platform parties, faculty, and graduating classes into university arenas with majestic pageantry.

approach

I coordinate with university protocol officers and stage managers to time the academic procession precisely to the length of the faculty line. The repertoire balances stately Scottish marches with uplifting celebratory fanfares suited for large arena acoustics.
